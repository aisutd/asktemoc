Title: URGENT: COVID-19 Protocols for Students Traveling to the U.S. from International Destinations - University Housing | The University of Texas at Dallas
Depth: 3

------------------------------------------------------------

We look forward to welcoming you to UTD Housing for the fall semester.  To have a seamless move-in when you arrive to campus, we would like to address the guidelines we are following for students who are arriving from an international destination to their new home.  We have procedures for vaccinated and non-vaccinated students, so please read the section that applies to you very carefully.

All residents with a housing assignment will be allowed to move to campus based on their assigned move-in date.
We will take steps to confirm when you arrived in the U.S. to determine a testing time frame.
All international students or students arriving from an international destination will be required to be tested with a viral test three to five days after arrival to the U.S. 

Vaccinated Students

Hours and days of the testing site on campus will be provided to you at move in.
Testing is free on campus; however, it is not free if you choose test off campus.
Test results take 48 hours, and students will be notified via their UT Dallas email or phone call from our COVID testing team if their results are positive. 
Students will self-monitor for COVID-19 symptoms; isolate and get tested again if they develop symptoms.
You are advised to follow all state and local recommendations or requirements after travel.

Non-Vaccinated Students

If you are a non-vaccinated individual, you will be required to isolate for seven days upon arriving to your housing unit.
Hours and days of the testing site on campus will be provided to you at move-in.
Testing is free on campus; however, it is not free if you choose test off campus.
Test results take 48 hours, and students will be notified via their UT Dallas email or phone call from our COVID testing team if their results are positive. 
If a student tests negative, they will continue to self-isolate for a full seven days from their arrival to the U.S.
If a student’s test is positive, they must remain isolated for a minimum of ten days from the date of the positive results to protect others from becoming infected.
If necessary, we will relocate students who test positive to units set aside for isolation.

Guidance from our COVID-19 Testing Manager

Starting on August 1st, you can access the appointment scheduler (Step 3 below) to schedule a testing time that is 3 to 5 days after arrival in the US. To maintain proper social distancing and cleaning protocols, please do not arrive more than 5 minutes before your scheduled time.

UTD has partnered with UTSW and LifePoint to manage the proactive testing process and receive results as quickly as possible. Once fully enrolled in the program (see steps below), the time to complete the actual testing process will be minimal.

Please complete the steps below to be fully enrolled in the testing program. Steps 1 and 2 must be completed 48 hours before you report for your first testing date.

Complete the online enrollment and consent form (https://veoci.com/veoci/p/form/rphf5ysn5qgd) Note: You must click submit entry once you have signed the form electronically.
Please review this instructional video to learn how to complete the self-administered nasal swab test – (https://vimeo.com/469344752/d52b4fbda3) Following the proper technique will reduce any inconclusive reports that will require employees to repeat the test for that week.
Schedule an appointment using the Testing Appointment Scheduler. (https://bit.ly/3itV7gz)
When you are tested, you will receive an email from Lifepoint to set up your patient portal. This is how you will receive your test results. Please follow the directions to complete set up so you will have access to your testing results. Results will NOT be sent separately to your UTD email.
You will receive your results within 48 hours of the test via LifePoint.  If the test is positive, a member of UTD’s C19 team will also contact you to begin the campus tracing process and determine possible next steps.

We look forward to seeing you this fall.
