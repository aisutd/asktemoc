## Degrees & Certificates
From the arts and sciences to engineering and management, UT Dallas’ degree programs are honed to produce world-class graduates.
[ View Majors and Degrees ](https://academics.utdallas.edu/degrees/)
[ Apply To UT Dallas ](https://enroll.utdallas.edu/apply/)
UT Dallas offers a variety of academic and professional certificates, including teacher certification programs.
[ See Certificate Options ](https://academics.utdallas.edu/certificates/)
[ Apply for Certificates ](https://academics.utdallas.edu/apply-for-certificates/)
### Two Paths for Teacher Certification
[UTeach Dallas](http://uteach.utdallas.edu/): For STEM majors seeking certification to teach middle and high school math, science or computer science.
[Teacher Development Center](https://teach.utdallas.edu/): For students and graduates from any academic discipline seeking Texas teacher certification.
